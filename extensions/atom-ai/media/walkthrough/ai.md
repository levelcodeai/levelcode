### Native Claude, built in

Atom++ ships Claude as a first-class part of the editor — not a bolted-on extension:

- **Chat** in the side bar (`⌥⌘I`) with your files as context
- **Inline completions** (ghost text) as you type
- **Edit with diff** — select code, `⌥⌘E`, review the change
- An **agent** that reads, searches, edits across files, runs commands, and verifies its own work

**Bring your own key or subscription.** Atom++ is BYOK — you connect your Anthropic API key (or sign in to your Atom++ account), and you're never locked in.
