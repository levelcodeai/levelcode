### Your setup, everywhere

Sign in once and Atom++ keeps your environment in sync across machines:

- **Settings, keybindings, snippets, and theme**
- **MCP servers** and **skills**
- Powered by the editor's own battle-tested sync engine, pointed at your Atom++ account

Your **API key never syncs** — it stays in your OS keychain. Open a fresh machine, sign in, and it's the editor you already know.
